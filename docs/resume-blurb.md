## Future Wording After V1 Exists

> Built a minimal agent-native social feed and single-agent AI red-team harness that generated fictional agent activity, replayed object-authorization, abuse, and prompt-injection scenarios, tracked findings, shipped fixes, and locked regressions with tests and public safety checks.

## Avoid Until Evidence Exists

- Do not call the app production-ready.
- Do not claim real users, real platform data, or a live social network.
- Do not describe it as a human-grade Twitter/X clone.
- Do not claim completed hardening before findings, fixes, and regression tests exist.
- Do not present synthetic coverage as proof of comprehensive security.
- Do not imply a 10-agent pentest or swarm benchmark; V1 is deliberately scoped to one adversarial runner.
